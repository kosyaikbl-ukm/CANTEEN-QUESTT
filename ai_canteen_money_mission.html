Ready for your canteen mission?

You have just finished your lesson and you're hungry. You have RM10, but you can spend only RM6. Order a meal and drink, ask about the cost, pay, calculate your change, and explain how much you save — all by speaking in English.

  • 1Look at the canteen menu and prepare what to say.
  • 2Talk to the virtual seller using your microphone or type if microphone access isn't available.
  • 3Keep your purchase at RM6 or less, pay with RM10 and say the correct change.
  • 4Explain how much you save, review your feedback and try again!
Prototype notice: This is a locally running rule-based virtual seller, not a connection to ELSA or a live AI model. Speech recognition depends on your browser and may use its own speech service. The feedback is an educational estimate, not a validated AI fluency assessment.
